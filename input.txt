CubeFlow - the system being simulated
=====================================

1. Overview

The warehouse is a cube storage system, a grid-based AS/RS. Stock is kept in
bins that are stacked directly on top of each other inside an aluminium grid,
with no aisles and no space between the stacks. Robots drive on the top of the
grid and reach the bins from above.

2. Robots

- Small rectangular robots, each one grid cell in size.
- Two sets of wheels at right angles: one set drives along X, the other along Y.
  To change direction the robot stops and lowers the other set (a pneumatic
  shift). It never turns or drives diagonally, only straight X or Y moves.
- A robot always occupies exactly one cell while driving.

3. Digging

Each robot has a cavity with a winch and a gripper on four bands. It parks over
a stack, lowers the gripper and latches onto the top bin.

Only the top bin of a stack can be lifted. If the bin you want is fifth from
the top, the robot first lifts bins 1 to 4 one at a time and sets each one down
on a neighbouring stack. Then it can lift bin 5.

After that the moved bins have to go somewhere. Either a robot puts them back
on the original stack in their original order, or they stay where they are and
the grid slowly re-sorts itself as bins come and go. The simulator should
support both.

4. Traffic

- Robots reserve the cells ahead of them before they drive into them. Path
  planning can use A*, or something like conflict-based search.
- A robot that is lifting or lowering a bin blocks its cell for everyone until
  the gripper is back up.
- Bins are delivered to ports on the edge of the grid. The robot lowers the bin
  into the port, and a person (or a picking robot) takes items out or puts them
  in.
