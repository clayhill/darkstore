DarkStore - tilt-tray parcel sorter
===================================

Read hub.txt first: the parcels, inducts, chutes and bags are those of
every parcel sorter. Implemented as TrayLoop (trayloop.html, js/tray/).

1. Overview

Trays on a chain run round a loop past the chutes; at the right chute a
tray tilts and the parcel slides off.

2. Layout

The loop is two straights joined by half circles; chutes on the outside of
both straights, the inducts at the start of the top one, fed by a belt from
the inbound dock.

3. Equipment

Trays 1 m apart at 2 m/s. An induct loads the next empty tray passing it.

4. Sorting

A tray passing one of its parcel's chutes tips it in if the bag has room.
Otherwise the parcel goes round again (recirculation); after three laps it
goes to the reject chute. A breakdown stops the whole loop.

5. Output

As hub.txt. The control room's fleet chart, table and map show the trays:
carrying, going round again, to the reject chute, or empty.

6. Parameters

2 m/s, trays 1 m apart, 3 laps before reject.

With the defaults about 2–3 % of parcels go round again. Its capacity is
one tray per metre of loop at its speed: about 7000 parcels an hour.
