CubeFlow - stacker crane AS/RS
==============================

Read common.txt first.

1. Overview

High-bay racking with aisles. In each aisle runs one stacker crane: a mast on
a floor rail, with a carriage that travels up and down the mast and a load
handler (telescopic forks or a tote handler) that reaches into the rack on
both sides. At the end of the aisle is an input/output point (I/O point)
where loads come in and go out, linked by conveyor to the workstations.

Two sizes, same model:

- mini-load: totes or trays, racks up to about 25 m
- unit-load: pallets, racks up to 40 m and more

For goods-to-person picking the pallet or tote goes to a pick station and
comes back. A unit-load system can also just send full pallets to shipping.

2. Layout

- A location is [aisle][side][bay][level][depth]. Depth is 1 (single deep),
  2 (double deep), or more with a satellite vehicle that drives into a
  channel.
- Each aisle has one I/O point, at floor level at the front, with separate
  in and out positions. Sometimes there is a second one at the back or at a
  higher level.

3. Equipment

- The crane drives along the aisle and moves the carriage up or down at the
  same time. So the travel time to a location is the larger of the two:
  t = max(t_horizontal, t_vertical), each with its own acceleration, speed and
  braking.
- After arriving there is a short settling time before the forks go out.
- The load handler takes a fixed time per fork cycle, longer for the back
  position in double deep.
- A mini-load handler can often hold two totes, so one trip can do two
  retrievals or two stores.
- Powered by rail, no battery.
- Variant: an aisle-changing crane that serves several aisles through a curve
  in the rail. Changing aisle costs time, and only one crane can be in a
  curve at a time.

4. Retrieval

The basic unit of work is a crane cycle:

- single command: from the I/O point to one location and back, storing or
  retrieving one load.
- dual command: from the I/O point, store a load at one location, drive to a
  second location, retrieve a load, return. Saves a lot of empty travel.
- with a two-tote handler: up to two stores and two retrievals in one cycle.

The dispatcher should make dual command cycles whenever both the store queue
and the retrieve queue have work, and pick the retrieval that is closest (in
crane time) to the store location.

Double deep: same relocation rule as the shuttle. If the front load is in the
way, move it to a free location in the same aisle first.

The standard way to check crane cycle times is FEM 9.851. The simulator can
report the FEM reference cycle next to the measured one, as a sanity check.

5. Traffic

- One crane per aisle, so cranes never meet.
- With two cranes in one aisle, they can't pass each other. Split the aisle
  into zones, or plan with a safety distance.
- Aisle-changing cranes reserve the curve and the target aisle.
- The I/O point has a small number of positions. When the outfeed conveyor is
  full the crane can't drop its load and has to wait.

6. Storage policy

- Use crane time, not distance, to rank locations. Because the crane moves in
  both directions at once, the best locations form a rectangle near the I/O
  point, not a strip along the floor.
- Class-based storage: A items in the fastest zone, then B, then C.
- Alternatives to compare: closest open location, and random.
- Spread each SKU over aisles, the same as in the shuttle spec.

7. Output

On top of the common output:

- Cycles per hour per crane, split into single command, dual command and
  relocation.
- Mean cycle time, measured and FEM 9.851 reference.
- Crane utilization.
- Relocation ratio (double deep).
- Queue lengths at the I/O points.

8. Parameters

Mini-load:
  aisles              4
  rack                60 bays x 15 levels per side, 0.65 m bays,
                      0.40 m levels
  load                tote 600 x 400 mm, up to 50 kg
  horizontal          5 m/s, 2 m/s2
  vertical            3 m/s, 3 m/s2
  settling            0.5 s
  handler             4 s per fork cycle, holds 2 totes

Unit-load:
  aisles              3
  rack                50 bays x 10 levels per side, 1.4 m bays,
                      1.8 m levels
  load                pallet 1200 x 800 mm, up to 1000 kg
  horizontal          3 m/s, 0.5 m/s2
  vertical            1 m/s, 0.5 m/s2
  settling            1 s
  handler             10 s per fork cycle, holds 1 pallet
