DARKSTORE Source on GitHub

Warehouse automation simulator

DarkStore

Lights-out warehouses, simulated. Pick a system and watch robots, operators and orders move through it in real time.

// Automated storage

Machines in fixed racking bring totes and pallets to the stations.

Cube storage · AS/RS

CubeFlow

Bins stacked in a dense grid. Robots on top dig down for the one they need.

Shuttle AS/RS

RailRunner

A shuttle on every level of the racking; lifts bring the totes down to the stations.

Stacker crane AS/RS

SkyStack

One tall crane per aisle drives and climbs at once to fetch each tote.

Pallet shuttle AS/RS

DeepLane

Pallets in deep lanes. On every level a carrier sends its satellite in for the front one.

// Mobile robots

Robots that drive the warehouse floor, with or without pickers.

Pod robots · goods-to-person

PodSwarm

Robots slide under shelving pods and carry the whole shelf to a pick station.

Case-handling robots

StackPilot

Robots with a tall mast collect a batch of totes from narrow aisles in one trip.

Climbing robots

RackRider

Robots drive into the aisle, climb the rack to a tote and bring it to the station.

Robots alongside pickers

AisleMate

Ordinary shelving: robots carry the order totes and pickers walk over to fill them.

// Order flow

Conveyors, sorters and whole buildings, where the order moves through.

Pharmacy wholesale

ZoneHop

Pharmacy order totes collect fast movers under an A-frame, then visit only the zones they need.

Parcel sorter

SortHub

Small robots carry each parcel to the chute for its destination. Or try a tilt-tray loop.

Multi-storey fulfilment

FloorStack

Storage, picking and shipping on three floors, joined by spirals and lifts that set the pace.

Fashion pocket sorter

PocketFlow

Every item rides alone in a pocket overhead; returns wait there for the next order, a sequencer drops each order in turn.

// Beyond the warehouse

The same storage, lifts and robots, moving something other than stock.

Automated car park

RoboValet

Drive into a cabin and walk away: lifts and flat robots park your car and bring it back facing the street.
DarkStore is a simulation and teaching tool, not control software for real systems · Apache-2.0
