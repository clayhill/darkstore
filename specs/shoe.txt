DarkStore - sliding shoe parcel sorter
======================================

Read hub.txt first: the parcels, inducts, chutes and bags are those of
every parcel sorter. Implemented as ShoeLine (shoeline.html, js/shoe/).

1. Overview

A straight line of slats, each with a shoe that can glide across it. At a
chute of its destination the shoes under a parcel glide diagonally across
the slats one after the other and push it off onto the chute's slide. The
most common high-speed sorter in parcel and e-commerce hubs.

Inspired by sliding shoe sorters in general (Vanderlande, Intelligrated,
Dematic, Interroll and others); not any one product.

2. Layout

Positions in metres: x along the line, y across it.

- The inducts north of the line before its start, each with a belt of its
  own that runs down to the merge.
- The line: from the merge, after a short run-in, a chute every 1.6 m on
  both sides; at its end the reject chute straight ahead.
- The recirculation belt from the end of the line back round behind the
  south chutes to the merge.
- Bag workers' aisles along both sides, joined round the far end.

3. Equipment

- Slats at 2 m/s; the shoes take 0.45 s to push a parcel across.
- Parcels 0.3–0.75 m long.
- Induct belts and the recirculation belt 1.5 m/s, accumulating.

4. Sorting

The merge (a gapper) lets one parcel at a time onto the sorter when the
last one is at least the gap (0.35 m) ahead of it: one coming round on the
recirculation belt first, else the one that has waited longest at the end
of an induct belt. A parcel crossing a chute of its destination with room
is pushed off. One that reaches the end of the line goes onto the
recirculation belt (a lap); after three laps, or with no room on that
belt, it goes into the reject chute straight ahead. With the reject bag
full a reject goes round too; if neither can take it the line stops until
one can. A breakdown stops the line.

5. Output

As hub.txt, and: how much of the line the parcels and their gaps take up,
the merge's load against the capacity the gaps allow, parcels going round,
time the line stood still.

6. Parameters

2 m/s, gap 0.35 m, chutes 1.6 m apart, 0.45 s to push off, 3 laps before
reject.

Its capacity is the speed over each parcel's length plus the gap: with
the defaults about 8200 parcels an hour in theory, and about 7000 in
practice, as the merge waits for a gap. A gap of 0.2 m or 2.5 m/s gives
about 8200–8400. With the defaults 3–5 % of parcels go round again.
