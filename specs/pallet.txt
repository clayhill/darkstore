DarkStore - pallet shuttle store
================================

Read common.txt first. Implemented as DeepLane.

1. Overview

Storage is a block of racking with deep lanes: each lane holds several
pallets one behind the other, on both sides of a transfer aisle that runs
along the front of the lanes on every level. A carrier (mother shuttle) runs
along each level's transfer aisle with a satellite (child shuttle) on board.
At a lane the satellite drives in on the lane's rails, under the pallets,
lifts one off the rails and brings it back onto the carrier. At the front of
the block a lift moves pallets between the levels and the pallet conveyors
that feed the stations.

This is the densest way to store pallets: no aisle per lane, only one
transfer aisle per level. It suits few SKUs with many pallets each (food,
drinks, consumer goods), and the price is access: in a lane only the pallet
nearest the transfer aisle can be taken.

2. Layout

- A position is [block][level][side][lane][position in the lane], the
  position counted from the transfer aisle (0 = the front pallet).
- One SKU per lane. A lane is filled from the back: a pallet put away goes
  in front of the others, so a lane works last in, first out.
- The two lanes nearest the lift on each side of a level hold the level
  buffers instead of pallets: short conveyors between the carrier and the
  lift, outbound on the left, inbound on the right (as in RailRunner).
- In front of the blocks a one-way pallet conveyor loop (the pre-zone, as
  in RailRunner) connects the lifts with the stations. Full pallets join it
  from the receiving dock on its left; empty pallets leave it to the
  empty-pallet stacker on its right.

3. Equipment

Carrier
  - One per level of each block, rail powered, runs along the transfer
    aisle: acceleration and braking, plus a fixed time to line up with a
    lane.
Satellite
  - Rides on its carrier and drives into the lanes on their rails, with its
    own acceleration and braking, and a fixed time to lift a pallet off the
    rails or set it down.
Lift
  - One per block, one pallet, with a platform on each side in line with
    the buffers and the block's conveyors. Acceleration and braking, a
    transfer time at each end. Usually the bottleneck.
Conveyor
  - Fixed speed; pallets keep their distance and give way where lines meet.

4. Retrieval

1. Only the front pallet of a lane can be reserved for an order line, and
   not while a pallet is on its way into that lane.
2. An order line the front pallets cannot fill on their own takes all a
   front pallet has left (it then leaves empty) and goes on with the
   pallet behind it. That pallet is fetched once the one in front has gone.
3. The carrier drives to the lane, the satellite fetches the pallet, the
   carrier drives to the front and the satellite sets it down in the
   outbound buffer (waiting while the buffer is full).
4. The lift brings it down to the conveyor, which takes it to the station.

5. Storage policy

- A returning pallet goes in front of pallets of its own SKU, or into an
  empty lane, never in front of a pallet a station is waiting for.
- Lanes its SKU already has are preferred, so a SKU does not spread over
  many half-empty lanes.
- Velocity-based: fast movers in the lanes nearest the lift, spread over
  the blocks and levels with the least work queued (as in RailRunner).
- A pallet picked empty does not go back: it leaves the loop to the
  empty-pallet stacker.

6. Inbound

Received goods arrive as new full pallets at the receiving dock (as many as
the receipt needs, each as full as a pallet holds). The conveyor takes each
one past the goods-in station, where the operator checks and labels it, and
on into the store. Replenishment orders whole pallets: below half a pallet,
two more.

7. Output

On top of the common output:

- Lift utilization and carrier cycles per hour.
- Pallets stored against positions, pallets received and pallets emptied.

8. Parameters

These are the defaults in js/pallet/config.js. A real store is usually
larger (more levels, longer and deeper lanes); these values keep the 3D view
readable.

  blocks               2
  levels               6, 1.9 m apart
  lanes                12 per side (1.25 m apart), the two front lanes of
                       each side hold the level buffers
  lane depth           6 pallets (1.3 m apart)
  pallet               1200 x 800 mm, 900 L of goods
  carrier              2.0 m/s, 0.5 m/s2, 1.0 s to line up with a lane
  satellite            1.0 m/s, 0.5 m/s2, 2.5 s to lift or set down
  level buffer         2 pallets out (left), 2 pallets in (right)
  lift                 1 pallet, 1.5 m/s, 0.8 m/s2, 6 s per transfer
  conveyor             0.5 m/s
  stations             3 pick, 1 goods-in, 3 pallets in flight per station
  orders               70 per hour

Not modelled yet: lanes reachable from both ends (first in, first out),
carriers that change level, full-pallet orders that leave without picking.
