DarkStore - parcel sorting: what the three sorters share
========================================================

Read common.txt first, but note: a sorter has no stock and no orders. Of
common.txt only the time model, the control room, the disruptions and the
barcodes apply. sort.txt (SortHub, a robot sorter), tray.txt (TrayLoop, a
tilt-tray loop) and shoe.txt (ShoeLine, a sliding shoe sorter) each add
their own machine to what is described here; code in js/hub/.

1. Overview

A parcel hub. Parcels arrive in a stream (from trucks, or from the packing
stations of a fulfilment centre), each labelled for one destination: a
route, a postcode area, a carrier depot. Operators at induction stations
scan each parcel and put it on the sorter, which drops it into the chute
for its destination. Every chute ends in a bag or cage; people change full
bags. Labels the scanner cannot read go to a reject chute, where someone
handles them by hand.

All three sorters sort the same stream with the same inducts, chutes and
bags, so with the same settings and seed their figures compare directly.

2. Layout

- Destinations: a list with a share of the volume each; a few take most of
  it (falling off with rank, 1 / rank^0.8).
- Chutes: one per destination, a second (third, ...) for the busiest ones,
  and the last one for rejects. With fewer chutes than destinations the
  rest go to the reject chute.
- Each sorter places its chutes and inducts its own way.

3. Equipment

- Induct: an operator takes a parcel off the feed belt and scans it (2.4 s),
  and scans the next while the sorter takes the last one.
- Bags of 30 parcels. Bag workers walk to a full bag and change it (25 s);
  each full bag gets the nearest free one.

4. Flow

Arriving parcels (Poisson) go to the induct with the shortest feed belt;
when every feed belt is full (40 parcels) they wait at the inbound dock. A
label the scanner cannot read (2 %) sends the parcel to the reject chute.
A parcel whose chutes are all full waits, goes round again or overflows,
as its sorter does.

5. Output

- Sort rate and induction rate per hour, parcels waiting to be inducted.
- Recirculation or overflow, rejects and no-reads.
- Bags changed, chutes full now, bag workers' utilization, inducts busy.
- Follow a parcel: from the feed belt through the induct and the sorter
  into its chute.

6. Parameters

- 3600 parcels/h, 28 destinations, 36 chutes, 4 inducts, 2 % no-reads.
- Bags of 30 parcels, 2 bag workers, 25 s per change, walking 1.2 m/s.
