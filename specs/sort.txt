DarkStore - robot parcel sorter
===============================

Read hub.txt first: the parcels, inducts, chutes and bags are those of
every parcel sorter. Implemented as SortHub (sorthub.html, js/sort/).

1. Overview

Small robots on a raised deck each carry one parcel from an induct to a
hole above its chute's bag and tip it in.

2. Layout

A deck of cells (0.75 m) with a hole every third cell in both directions,
the induct lanes along the front, chargers and parking along the back.
Bags hang under the holes.

3. Equipment

Robots: about 0.6 m square, 2 m/s, quick to accelerate and turn, a tilt
tray on top (0.8 s to tip), battery with charging.

4. Sorting

A robot queues at an induct, takes the parcel, and drives to the nearest
chute of its destination with room. With every one of them full the parcel
goes to the overflow (reject) chute. A chute that fills up while the robot
is on its way is chosen again on arrival; with nowhere to go at all the
robot waits in the parking row.

5. Traffic

- One-way streets between the holes: of the two rows between hole rows one
  runs east and one west, of the two columns between hole columns one runs
  south and one north.
- No stopping inside a crossing: a robot only drives into one when it can
  drive on out of it.
- Inducts are pass-throughs: robots come down a southbound street into the
  lane and leave straight on onto a northbound one. No more robots head for
  an induct than its lane holds and a few on their way; robots with nothing
  to do park along the back.

6. Output

As hub.txt, with overflow instead of recirculation, and the robots' time
split and battery.

7. Parameters

45 robots, 2 m/s, 2 m/s² acceleration, 0.35 s to turn, 10 chargers. The
default deck takes up to about 70 robots; more crowd the streets.

With the defaults it keeps up with the 3600 parcels an hour; 45 robots
manage about 3800 an hour, however many parcels come.
